Historical Introduction

In 1994, over the course of roughly one hundred days, more than one million Tutsi were killed in the Genocide against the Tutsi in Rwanda. This page is dedicated to remembering those who were lost, honoring survivors, and presenting verified historical context and testimony with accuracy and respect.
