About StoryCast — Remembering 1994

StoryCast is a student-built educational microsite dedicated to remembrance and historical education about the 1994 Genocide against the Tutsi in Rwanda. It supports the broader tradition of Kwibuka ("to remember" in Kinyarwanda), Rwanda's annual commemoration period. This page describes the project's mission, planned accessibility features, and current status honestly — nothing below should be read as a claim of completed verification or testing.

Mission Statement (Draft)

Pillar I — Accuracy

Historical Accuracy & Primary Sources

Goal: verify Kinyarwanda-language sources against primary documentation and recognized historical records (e.g. ICTR and Gacaca court records) before publishing any account.

II

Pillar II — Ethics

Trauma-Informed Ethical Storytelling

Planned practice: pre-interview consent review, ongoing consent checks, and clear content warnings for sensitive survivor material.

III

Pillar III — Access

Open & Universally Accessible Knowledge

Goal: no paywalls, open educational licensing, and synchronized transcripts for audio/video once real recordings are available.

Accessibility & Sensory Review

Working Toward WCAG 2.1 AA

StoryCast aims to follow the W3C Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA as closely as possible. This has not been formally tested yet; see docs/accessibility-checklist.md for current status.

Standard 1.2.3 · Planned

Synchronized Captions & Audio Description

Planned: every video will include synchronized captions in English and Kinyarwanda, with non-verbal cues explicitly annotated for Deaf and hard-of-hearing visitors.

Standard 1.2.1 · Planned

Verbatim Transcripts

Planned: a full spoken transcript readable alongside audio, with a plain-text and accessible-PDF export once real media exists.

Standard 2.1.1 · Implemented

Keyboard & Screen Reader Flow

Implemented: a logical reading order, a working skip-to-content link, and fully native, keyboard-operable controls — including the Text Scale and Accessibility Mode toggles on the Story Detail page — with no custom JavaScript keyboard handling.

Standard 1.4.8 · Partly implemented

Sensory Adjustments

Implemented: a CSS-only Readability Font Mode and Text Scale control, plus reduced-motion support site-wide. Low-bandwidth media formats are planned once real audio/video is added.
